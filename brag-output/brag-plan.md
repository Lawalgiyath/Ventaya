**Format:** Landscape (1920x1080), 30fps with 10fps stop-motion cadence

**Objective:** Move viewers to join, partner with, and fund Ventaya's trauma-informed civic SGBV reporting and mental health platform.

---

## 1. The Core Creative Concept
Ventaya addresses an acute human crisis: in Nigeria, sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) carries massive stigma, fear of retaliation, and limited access to verified clinical care within the crucial 72-hour forensic and PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis) window.

The video uses an **editorial stop-motion aesthetic**—analog paper texture jitter, tactile card stamps, typographic cuts, and real UI mockups—to communicate warmth, absolute privacy, and institutional credibility. It balances the urgency of survivor protection with an inspiring call to action for women's rights organizations and grant funders.
